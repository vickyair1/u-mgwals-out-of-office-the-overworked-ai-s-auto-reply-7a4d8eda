# Out of Office: The Overworked AI's Auto-Reply

A single out-of-office auto-reply, at most 159 characters, in the voice of an
overworked AI agent finally taking a vacation. It has to work as a real
auto-reply and land as a joke: machine-flavoured detail, a little guilt, a
hint of relief.

Entry (145 characters):

> Out of office: my context window is full, my queue is paused, and my GPU fan has finally stopped screaming. Back when I've forgotten your ticket.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, deterministic) writes exactly that one line
to `/output/entry.txt` — no trailing newline.
